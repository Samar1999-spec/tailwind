A creative + tech studio, run on directed AI

Train the AI before you ask it to perform.

Large language models behave like method actors — they do their best work when you direct them with role, circumstances, objective, and motivation. Questions

Before you apply.

Ch. 01

Do I need to be technical?

+

No. The method is a directing procedure — nine plain questions and a set of backbones. Engineers, actors, writers, founders, and problem-solvers each get a track tuned to their work.

Ch. 02

What's actually in the membership?

+

A directed prompt library for your track, six master backbones, an agent suite that chains into workflows, a community, and — on higher tiers — a verified member registry and business tools.

Ch. 03

Why an application and an assessment?

+

A closed studio stays valuable only if the room stays sharp. Stage 1 measures reasoning and character, not polish, so the community and registry mean something.

Ch. 04

Where does the method come from?

+

From the step-by-step techniques of Stanislavski, Meisner, Strasberg, Adler, Chekhov, and Uta Hagen.

Ch. 05

Can I cancel anytime?

+

Yes. Membership is month-to-month, or annual at a lower rate. Your free-tier access remains.
