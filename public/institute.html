Membership

Admission is free and permanent — the paper you sat cannot be taken away. What a plan buys is depth: the library, the teaching, the company, and eventually the right to teach here yourself.

Plans are selected here and billed at launch. No payment details are taken on this page.

Institutional membership

Studios, agencies, theatres, production companies and schools join as an institution rather than as individuals: seats for a whole department, a private classroom under the institution's own name, and Studio OS against a live production. Institutional terms are agreed directly with the studio.

Classrooms

A classroom is a room a member runs: their discipline, their syllabus, their seats, their fee. The institute supplies the framework, the agent suite and the floor; the founder supplies the craft.

The directory

Listings shown are illustrative openings across the five founding disciplines. Rooms you found are added to the top of this directory.

Found a classroom

Open to actors, writers, directors, producers, and to businesses — agencies, theatres, production companies, schools. Founding requires the Founder plan, which carries the agent suite that runs the room.

Four departments

Each department teaches the same framework against a different instrument. You are admitted to one and may audit the rest.

The production standard

Every paper, prompt system and note issued by the institute is authored and signed off only by actors, writers and directors with ten or more years of professional experience. Craft governs the syllabus. No prompt reaches a candidate unsigned.

The Rehearsal Floor

Most prompting stops at the brief. The last four elements of the framework are what make it a rehearsal: a first take, an honest note, a refinement, and memory carried forward. Select a stage.

Four terms

The ladder is the same in every department and in every classroom founded here. Each term ends at a gate — a piece of work a senior creative signs or sends back.

Two wings, one engine

The institute is the teaching floor between the studio's two product lines. What you build here goes out through one of them.

Creator wing

Method Master Prompts

A complete personal AI system, not a prompt pack. Your dossier is the first artefact; everything below is built on it.

  • Custom prompt systems per track
  • Education and training plan
  • Production workflows and app integration
  • Portfolio development and ongoing notes
  • Career and business strategy
Production wing

Studio OS

Production intelligence for filmmaking — the same method-prompt architecture applied to a whole shooting company rather than one practitioner.

  • Script breakdown and the production graph
  • Scheduling, stripboard and change propagation
  • Budgeting and cost deltas
  • Logistics and post metadata
  • Compliance ledger and consent records

The agent suite

The Founder plan grants a working team rather than a single assistant. Each agent is briefed from your dossier and signed off against the department's papers.

The right door

The institute does not teach the generation of finished content. It teaches direction — how a practitioner briefs, critiques and carries a performance forward. Infrastructure and capability, not imitation.
