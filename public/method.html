The Methodin full

Train the AI before you ask it to perform.

Large language models behave like method actors — they perform best when you direct them with role, circumstances, objective, and motivation. This is the system that turns acting's oldest disciplines into a modern prompting method for your creative, technical, and entrepreneurial work.

Start with the framework Meet the agents

“Given the circumstances, do the truth.”Konstantin Stanislavski

An LLM doesn't reason like a mathematician — it performs like an actor. A prompt is a script and a cue; the response is a performance. Direct the performance and the results change.

The idea, honestly sourced The method-actor framing comes from Colin Doyle's paper “LLMs as Method Actors” (2024). Method Machine Studio builds on it — replacing his engineering principles with the step-by-step preparation of real acting masters, so anyone can direct, not just engineers.

The shared output contract
Take→Critique→Adjust→Continuity

Every agent, whatever its method, delivers a first take, finds its own weakest point, fixes it, and states what carries forward. One agent's Continuity becomes the next one's brief — that's what makes them a company, not six unrelated prompts.
