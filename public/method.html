The agent suite

Every backbone is deployed as an agent. A Director classifies the task and routes it to the right specialist; each specialist runs one discipline and honours a shared output contract, so agents chain into production workflows.

Orchestrator

The Director

Routes rather than solves. The task is classified, the backbone and domain track are selected, a one-line brief is written and the work is handed off. Every request enters the suite here.

The shared output contract
Take→Critique→Adjust→Continuity

Every agent, whatever its discipline, delivers a first take, identifies its weakest point, corrects it and declares what carries forward. One agent's Continuity becomes the next agent's brief: a company, not six unrelated prompts.
